===========================================
Mn-SOD Comparative Genomics Project
===========================================

1. Species used:
   - Escherichia coli (reference)
   - Pseudomonas aeruginosa
   - Bacillus subtilis
   - Staphylococcus aureus
   - Salmonella enterica

2. Tools used:
   - NCBI BLAST (sequence retrieval)
   - Clustal Omega (Multiple Sequence Alignment)
   - FastTree (Phylogenetic tree)

3. Key Results:
   - E. coli and Salmonella are 99.9% identical (distance: 0.000000005)
   - Pseudomonas aeruginosa is the most distant (distance: 0.361)
   - Bacillus and Staphylococcus are moderately close (distance: 0.100 - 0.266)

4. Conserved Motif Identified:
   - YAYDALEPHID (metal-binding site)
   - Histidine residues at active site

5. Phylogenetic Tree (Newick format):
   (AZP68180.1:0.361497870,(UUF21481.1:0.024771997,ARO96817.1:0.000000005)1.000:0.388276492,(RUS08747.1:0.100849980,KKI65849.1:0.266683677)0.983:0.179175492);

6. Conclusion:
   - Mn-SOD is highly conserved among Enterobacteriaceae
   - Pseudomonas shows significant evolutionary divergence
   - The active site residues are conserved across all species
